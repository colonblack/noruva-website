For manufacturers entering a new market, a clearly appointed local representative can help maintain continuity, improve responsiveness and organise commercial engagement with relevant stakeholders. Noruva offers pharmaceutical representation support under a written mandate that defines products, territory, responsibilities and limits of authority.

Local commercial representation

Subject to the agreed mandate, Noruva can serve as a local point of coordination between the manufacturer and relevant market stakeholders. This may include managing enquiries, coordinating meetings, communicating approved product information, following up on commercial discussions and reporting market feedback to the manufacturer.

Market-development planning

We work with the manufacturer to establish practical priorities for the agreed territory and portfolio. Activities may include mapping target customer segments, identifying relevant institutions and potential channel partners, planning engagement, maintaining an opportunity pipeline and reviewing progress against agreed objectives.

Stakeholder engagement and relationship management

We coordinate professional engagement with potential distributors, wholesalers, hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, institutional buyers and other relevant stakeholders, depending on the product and mandate. Introductions and discussions are documented so both parties have a clear record of contacts, next steps and responsibilities.

Commercial opportunity coordination

Where a potential opportunity emerges, Noruva can help organise the exchange of requirements, approved product information, indicative commercial details and next steps. Quotations, supply commitments, product availability and contractual terms remain subject to confirmation by the manufacturer and the parties responsible for the transaction.

Reporting and feedback

Reporting may cover stakeholder engagement, enquiries, market observations, competitor information gathered within the agreed scope, pipeline status, barriers encountered and recommended next actions.

Exclusivity and scope

Exclusivity is not assumed. Where proposed, it must be expressly stated in a signed agreement, with clear products, territory, duration, performance expectations, exceptions, authority and termination provisions. Noruva does not claim to represent a manufacturer unless the appointment is confirmed in writing.

How the engagement works

1. Define the mandate

2. Build the activity plan

3. Develop relationships

Potential deliverables

  • Written representation scope and activity plan.
  • Stakeholder and opportunity pipeline.
  • Meeting and follow-up records.
  • Periodic market-development reports.
  • Commercial feedback and recommended next steps.

Who this service is for

Pharmaceutical manufacturers and healthcare-product companies seeking a structured local commercial presence or support developing relationships in the DRC.

Clear responsibilities

Noruva’s authority is limited to the written mandate. Unless separately agreed and appropriately authorised, representation does not include acting as importer of record, holding inventory, physically distributing products, providing medical advice or binding the manufacturer to a contract.
