Entering a new market requires a clear understanding of applicable regulatory requirements, product documentation, local responsibilities and the sequence of activities needed before a product can be lawfully marketed. Noruva supports manufacturers and their designated partners by coordinating the process, tracking documentation and facilitating communication with qualified regulatory professionals and relevant authorities.

Marketing Authorisation (AMM)

For pharmaceutical products, the Autorisation de Mise sur le Marché (AMM) is a key regulatory authorisation associated with the lawful marketing of a medicine in the DRC. Noruva can coordinate the administrative and project-management aspects of the AMM pathway for a manufacturer or applicant, subject to applicable rules and the responsibilities of the authorised applicant and qualified professionals.

Our coordination support may include mapping expected steps, preparing document-request lists, assigning responsibilities, tracking required materials, coordinating product samples or supporting documents where applicable, monitoring submissions and questions, and organising follow-up with the relevant parties.

CTD Dossier Coordination

We support the organisation and coordination of the Common Technical Document (CTD) dossier used for a medicine registration application, where required by applicable DRC guidance. The CTD is a structured format for presenting administrative and product information alongside quality, non-clinical and clinical documentation. The precise dossier content and presentation must follow current requirements of the competent authority and the product category.

Depending on the mandate, Noruva may help coordinate collection and tracking of materials for relevant CTD sections, maintain a dossier checklist and document register, identify missing or inconsistent items for responsible experts to review, coordinate translations or administrative records where required, and track versions and outstanding actions. Scientific content, technical assessments and regulatory declarations must be prepared or validated by the manufacturer and appropriately qualified professionals.

Regulatory pathway assessment

Before a project begins, we help establish an initial view of the likely regulatory workstream, key documentation needs, responsible parties, dependencies and open questions. This may include distinguishing product registration requirements from separate requirements relating to importation, establishment authorisation, labelling or other market activities.

Documentation and submission coordination

Regulatory projects often involve multiple parties and substantial documentation. Noruva can coordinate document requests, maintain a status tracker, organise correspondence, monitor agreed milestones and help ensure responsibilities and outstanding actions are visible to the client and its appointed experts.

Renewals, variations and lifecycle coordination

After an authorisation has been issued, product information or regulatory obligations may change. Where included in the mandate, Noruva can help track renewal dates, coordinate information required for variations or updates, maintain an action calendar and liaise with the manufacturer or appointed regulatory professionals on relevant post-authorisation activities.

Regulatory intelligence and local coordination

We help clients identify regulatory questions that need confirmation, monitor agreed issues and coordinate requests for clarification through appropriate channels. Advice on interpretation of legislation, scientific assessments or regulated professional matters should be provided by suitably qualified professionals where required.

How the process works

1. Initial assessment

2. Requirements mapping

3. CTD and document coordination

4. Submission and follow-up coordination

5. Lifecycle tracking

Potential deliverables

  • Initial market-entry and regulatory workstream summary.
  • AMM and CTD documentation checklist and responsibility matrix.
  • Document tracker, version log and outstanding-items register.
  • Milestone calendar and periodic status reports.
  • Coordination of meetings, correspondence and agreed follow-up actions.

Who this service is for

International pharmaceutical manufacturers, product owners and authorised applicants assessing or preparing to enter the DRC market, as well as organisations coordinating product-registration projects with qualified local professionals.

Important regulatory note

Noruva provides coordination and market-entry support within the agreed scope. Competent authorities determine whether an AMM or other authorisation is granted. Requirements, formats, fees, samples and timelines must be confirmed for the relevant product and application. Noruva does not guarantee approval, registration timelines or a favourable regulatory outcome.
